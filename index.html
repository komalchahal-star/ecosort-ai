DESIGN THINKING APPROACH

Five stages, one habit problem

  • Empathize — I asked people around me why sorting waste feels confusing and slow.
  • Define — The real problem: no quick way to check the correct bin in the moment.
  • Ideate — I explored a few ideas and landed on a conversational AI assistant.
  • Prototype — I designed a chat + image-based flow backed by a municipal-rules knowledge base.
  • Test & Refine — I reviewed sample chats with mentors and refined the tone.

RESPONSIBLE AI CONSIDERATIONS

Non-negotiables, not afterthoughts

Fairness

Rules sourced across urban and semi-urban contexts so advice isn't biased toward one city's system.

Transparency

Every answer states the reason for a bin choice, not just a bare instruction.

Ethics

The assistant only gives disposal guidance — it never collects or profiles user data.

Privacy

No personal or location data is stored; sessions are stateless.

EXPECTED IMPACT

Who benefits, and by how much

  • Households: spend less time guessing; build lasting sorting habits.
  • Municipalities: cleaner, better pre-sorted waste, cutting reprocessing cost.
  • Environment: less recyclable material lost to landfill.
  • Recycling workers: safer handling, fewer mixed hazardous items.
